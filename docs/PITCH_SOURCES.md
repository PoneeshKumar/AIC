# Sources for the pitch

Use these numbers exactly as worded. Say "out of range", not "too hot", because the best documented failure is freezing.

| Claim you can make | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| In published studies, 14% to 35% of fridges or transport shipments exposed vaccines to freezing temperatures | Matthias et al., Vaccine, 2007 (systematic review) | Peer reviewed |
| Across studies that followed the whole chain, 75% to 100% of shipments were exposed | Same review | Peer reviewed |
| A later update covering 45 studies found the problem continuing in wealthy and lower income countries alike | Hanson et al., Vaccine, 2017 | Peer reviewed |
| Insulin in patients' home fridges sat out of range about 11% of the time in a 388 patient study | Braune et al., presented at EASD 2018 | Conference research |
| The biopharma industry loses about $35 billion a year to failures in temperature-controlled logistics | Attributed to the IQVIA Institute, reported in trade press | Industry estimate, original report not checked |

Do not say "50% of vaccines are wasted because of temperature". The WHO figure covers all causes of wastage.

## Lines to stay behind

- Our shipment, product and budget are simulated. Say so.
- We do not quote storage temperatures or time limits for any real product.
- The agent never says a product is safe or unsafe to use.
